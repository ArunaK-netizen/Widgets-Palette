COSMO360 constellation photo library

88 unique 960 px WebP images are stored in images/: exactly one primary gallery photo for each of the 88 constellations. Additional detail-page photos and other objects photographed in the direction of constellations were excluded. The images are bundled in the Android app under app/src/main/assets/constellations/images/ for offline widget use.

Attributions and the source page for each image are recorded in attributions.json. The images are identified by COSMO360 as CC BY 4.0; retain the listed photographer/source credit and license link if any image is used or redistributed.
